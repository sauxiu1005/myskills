# 二维媒介与角色身份 / 2D medium and identity

## 中文方法
风格硬要求：明显的探形线、断线、复笔，疏松水粉上色。线稿主导造型：轮廓与关节转折保留可辨认的试探线和选择性重复笔触，线条有轻重、间断与不闭合处；色块概括、边缘松动、局部留白，不完全填满或覆盖线稿。人物与背景均执行，角色参考与视频提示都须明确表达这四点，不得仅写“欧式漫画风”。避免清稿式封闭轮廓、均匀描边、光滑满涂或用噪点划痕替代真实探形线；连续运动保留笔触但不以全画面随机闪烁制造手绘感。

1. 先造型比例与轮廓，再大明暗、区域配色，最后纹理。每个角色可选主几何形、局部比例反差、非对称和动作线；不统一头身比，不把夸张设计修成时装人体或标准动漫脸。
2. 保留可见结构线、轻重变化、断续轮廓、选择性复笔。水粉与干刷色块不完全盖线；人物背景共享同一媒介和细节层级。线密度服从结构，不用满屏噪点、均匀划线或粗黑描边冒充手绘。
3. 配色随剧情、场景、情绪选择并跨镜统一；不继承历史街景色号。避免无意变成光滑矢量或3D，但场景不限定城市。
4. 每片按剧本列角色表：身份、叙事职责、轮廓比例、脸与发型、服装、道具状态、参考来源及版本。既定角色可贯穿任何获准场景，固定身份不固定事件。
5. 有用户指定或已认可形象就复用；无形象才在确认制作范围后建立本片锚点。保持角色辨识所需部位清楚，必要时全身含脚，道具左右手以身体侧记录。关键真实人物事实缺失时不自行捏造。
6. 每次同角色生成实际绑定同一选定身份图，冻结确切参考版本；文字描述只补充，不能替代图输入。身份改变或用户选择另一版时更新依赖记录，不静默切换。
7. 身份参考负责脸、发型、设计比例、服装与当前道具状态；风格参考负责线、色块、媒介。分别声明用途，不把风格图中的演员、拼图布局或背景当作新场景。
8. 姿态可弯曲、扭转、压缩拉伸并恢复身份；不能以一致性为由冻结身体、手脚、构图、轮廓和镜头。
9. 明确每事件演员和道具数量，配角按故事选择，避免重复模板、镜像分身、凭空出现物体。前段末帧只在当前工作流需要且获准时作为状态参考，与身份图共同使用，不能把旁人当主角。
10. 道具入场、离手、换手、收起或交付都需可见过渡与出场状态；不沿用上一故事的标志道具或持握方式。

## English method
Required visual treatment: clearly visible searching/construction lines, broken contours, selective retraced strokes and loose gouache coloring. Linework leads the form: retain exploratory marks at contours and joint turns, varied weight, gaps and open edges. Use broad, loosely edged color patches with areas left unpainted; never fully fill or bury the drawing. Apply this to characters and backgrounds, and state all four traits in identity-reference and video prompts rather than relying on the label “European comic style.” Avoid cleaned-up closed outlines, uniform inking, smooth solid fills or random scratches/noise as substitutes for genuine form-searching marks. Preserve strokes in motion without random full-frame flicker.

Design silhouette/proportion first, broad values and regional palette second, texture last. Use individual geometric shapes, local proportional contrast, asymmetry and action lines; never impose a shared head count or normalize exaggeration into fashion anatomy or a standard anime face.
Keep structural lines, varied weight, broken contours and selective retracing visible under incompletely covering gouache/dry brush. Match character and background medium; avoid uniform scratches, noise and indiscriminate heavy outlines.
Choose palette for this story and keep it coherent across shots. No historical city palette, setting or cast is inherited.
Create a story-specific identity ledger. Reuse approved designs or establish anchors within confirmed production; actually bind the same selected image/version to later shots. Text alone is not identity guidance.
Separate identity from medium references. Do not leak reference backgrounds, bystanders or triptych layouts into shots. Relevant anatomy and prop states must be legible; record anatomical holding hands where needed.
Identity consistency permits acting deformation and recovery, not frozen poses. Declare actor/prop counts and distinguish supporting roles. Use prior actual frames only for an authorized continuity need, together with the identity anchor.
Track visible prop handoffs and state transitions; do not inherit an old story's signature props or events.
