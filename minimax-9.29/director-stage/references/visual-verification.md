# Visual verification / 视觉验收

The actual rendered scene is the evidence for appearance. Structural checks and metrics diagnose problems but do not approve composition.

## Still scene

Check structural consistency and explain intentional exceptions. Capture the affected view, open it, then inspect in order:

1. silhouette and primary-subject readability;
2. proportions, ground/seat contact and intersections;
3. facing, eyelines, interaction and accidental occlusion;
4. framing, headroom, negative space and lens distortion;
5. foreground/background separation and lighting readability;
6. whether the requested dramatic beat reads.

Name visible mismatches and correct the most consequential one first. Recapture the corrected view. Separate full-scene checks from isolated multi-view checks for an object's shape; neither replaces the other.

## Timeline

A static image cannot approve animation. Sample the start, middle and end of movement and action clips, just before and after camera cuts, and the middle of gaps. Inspect samples side by side in time order. A gap can leave a character frozen mid-stride, and an ending can release a bow before the intended closing image.

Use contact sheets to compare adjacent beats; inspect full-resolution frames when small contact points, thin props or distant faces are unclear. Look for sliding, clipping, sudden jumps, pose discontinuity and a subject leaving frame. Sampled images leave unsampled motion unverified; use playback when available and report what was actually reviewed.

“Captured” means a file was produced. “Inspected” means its image was opened and judged. “Saved” requires verified persistence. Report missing evidence, hidden geometry and remaining mismatches honestly, including when a bounded correction loop has ended.

## 中文要点

外观以实际渲染为证据，结构和指标只能辅助诊断。检查结构后截取并打开目标视角，依次看剪影与主次、比例与接触、互动朝向与遮挡、构图与透视、前后景与光线，最后判断戏剧节点是否清晰。优先修正最关键问题，再截图复查。整场景验收和物件多视图验收不能相互替代。

动画不能只凭静图验收。抽样检查移动与动作的开始、中段、结束，切镜前后及空档中段，并按时间并排比较。空档可能让人停在半步，结尾可能在需要保持鞠躬时提前直起。接触表先比较连续性，小接触点、细道具或远处脸部不清楚时再看原分辨率。检查滑步、穿插、跳变、姿态不连贯和出画；抽样不覆盖全部中间运动，能够播放时检查播放，并报告实际范围。

产出文件才叫“已截图”，打开并判断画面才叫“已看图检查”，确认持久化才叫“已保存”。如实列出缺失证据、隐藏结构和剩余差异，包括定向修正次数已用尽的情况。
