# Product identity references

Use this method to reduce ambiguity about a real product, not to produce a comprehensive card by default. Protect the relevant silhouette, proportions, components, material, markings and confirmed states using actual sources. This image serves as the product's consistency anchor. [Scene anchors](tvc-moodboard-creative-guide.md) develop the environment and art direction separately, without product or lettering consistency requirements.

## Choose the smallest useful preparation

| Available evidence | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Clear originals already cover the needed identity and views | Use them directly; make no new card |
| Product sources are too unclear to reliably recognize the required identity | Generate a product identity reference card from supported source views before creative generation; keep unsupported details unknown |
| Useful evidence is scattered and hard to use together | Arrange selected existing views into a clear identity card, retaining originals |
| A necessary angle or state is missing | Obtain or clarify the missing evidence, then supplement only that gap; use another composition if the unknown detail can be avoided |
| A mark, control or texture needs precise recognition | Keep a clear original or local crop at readable scale; do not shrink it into a dense grid |

An identity card can be an arrangement of existing sources, or a generated reference image when the available product material is too unclear for reliable recognition. In the latter case, generate only a source-grounded card before creative image generation; it does not authenticate an unsupported fact. Choose only views and details required by the planned work, with enough room for recognition. There are no required zones, view count or material swatches. If a needed fact remains unsupported, leave it unresolved and state the limitation rather than inventing it.

Unseen specifications, reverse-side markings and components remain unknown. A plausible generated view does not supply evidence for them. A source-backed change of view still needs verification for its intended use. An explicitly original concept may establish a coherent design, clearly distinguished from a real product's identity.

## When an image prompt is necessary

Describe the actual source, protected details, exact missing view or state, and the readable result needed in later shots. Use only the relevant parts of this optional scaffold:

```text
Prepare {one needed view / detail / compact identity card} for {subject and intended use}.
Use {actual source} to preserve {verified shape, proportions, components, marks and state}.
Show {specific evidence-backed coverage}; leave {unsupported details} unresolved.
Keep {recognition features or exact detail} large and unobscured enough to read.
Use lighting that reveals {relevant geometry or material} faithfully.
If arranging several sources, keep only complementary views and essential accurate labels.
```

Use available observation within the authorized review scope to check identity and legibility before relying on prepared references. Retain original high-resolution brand and packaging sources; a generated card does not authenticate new facts. If a view is wrong, retain the valid source and report the gap rather than automatically regenerating it.

Hand off the usable originals, crops or prepared image with their identity roles. Their incidental background, labels, layout and ordinary lighting do not constrain the advertising world. A clear original can remain the most useful final reference.
