# Visual Quality Reference / 视觉质感参考

## Use / 使用时机
Read for premium/cinematic, multi-character, material-sensitive, or direct text-layout requests. / 高品质、多人物、材质敏感或直出文字排版时读取。

## Prompt order / 提示词顺序
`subject/social relation -> world/set -> blocking/frame geometry -> shot/lens intent -> focus/depth -> light/exposure -> material response -> motion/sound -> product ending`
Every quality adjective must attach to a visible subject, surface, camera decision, or physical action. / 每个质感词必须绑定可见主体、表面、摄影决定或物理动作。

## Character separation / 人物区分
Lock role, age band, face impression, hair silhouette, costume cut/material, accessory, posture, screen position, and first entrance. Give each character a different silhouette and visual job. / 锁定身份、年龄段、面部气质、发型轮廓、服装剪裁材质、配饰、姿态、站位和首次出场；人物轮廓和画面职责必须不同。

## Material and light / 材质与光
Specify contact shadows, reflections, fabric weave, metal/plastic/glass response, food surface, skin, controlled highlights, restrained saturation, and motivated light direction. / 明确接触阴影、反射、织物经纬、金属塑料玻璃响应、食物表面、皮肤、受控高光、克制饱和度和有动机光向。

Realism overrides decorative grading. Every key, fill, rim, colored reflection, and haze must be explainable by a visible or scene-native source, with plausible direction, distance falloff, exposure, shadow softness, and material response. Treat palettes such as teal-orange as restrained grading and production-design guidance, not as permission to cast unrelated colored light over every face and surface. Avoid universal rim light, source-free glow or fog, oily highlights, plastic skin, crushed blacks, excessive HDR separation, and game-render sheen. / 真实感优先于装饰性调色。主光、补光、轮廓光、彩色反射和雾都必须能由可见或场景原生光源解释，并具有可信的方向、距离衰减、曝光、阴影软硬和材质响应。青橙等色调只作为克制调色与美术指导，不能把无来源彩光涂满人物和表面。避免全员轮廓光、无来源光晕或雾、油亮高光、塑料皮肤、死黑暗部、过度HDR分离和游戏渲染感。

## Ending / 结尾
Finish the plot before the hero shot. Use the reveal as a sound and lighting pivot, then hold the product long enough to read. Direct copy belongs to the hero composition, not a subtitle bar. / 商品镜头前先完成剧情；用揭晓完成声音与光线转调；商品停留足够阅读；直出文案属于英雄构图而非字幕条。
