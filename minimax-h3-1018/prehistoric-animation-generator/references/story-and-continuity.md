# 故事、样张与跨镜连续性 / Story, previews and continuity

## 无参考是完整输入分支 / Reference-free input is normal

主题或脚本足以设计新故事。不要把上传参考当作必填，也不自动读取旧角色。先拆角色职责、目标、关系与关键动作，再在通用二维史前画风内分别设计身份。用户明确指定外部参考或旧人时，记录其用途；否则参考输入为空。
A topic or script is sufficient. Do not require uploads or automatically read old characters. Derive roles, goals, relationships and actions, then design each identity within the shared style. Only explicitly designated external references or previous characters enter the input; otherwise it is empty.

## 提案模板 / Proposal template

- 完整短故事：目标→行动→障碍→后果→反应。
- 当前角色卡：使用对应语言的角色模板，各人脸型发型服装道具不同，符合剧情；乱发胡茬非必备。
- 时间分镜：时间、动作、接触反馈、运镜理由、短英文台词或无台词、音效与衔接。
- 样张计划：剧情需要的视图/表情/布局及数量，不固定角色、四格、表情列表或尺寸。
- 执行范围：确认视频后自动生成内部角色图并继续视频，生成成功直接交付。不设自动检查、独立样张审核或重复授权；仅用户明确先审图时等待。

The proposal includes a complete story, character cards, timed actions, camera, exact dialogue, sound and internal reference needs. After video confirmation, generate necessary character images and video continuously, then deliver directly. No automated inspection, default sample review or repeated authorization. Wait only for explicitly requested user review or real tool blockers. Document titles remain off screen.

## 内部角色图连续性 / Internal character-image continuity

内部角色图生成后直接用于已确认视频，本故事跨镜保留身份、服装和脸型；动作表情可变。新故事默认新设计。效果由用户检查，不自动返修。
Generated character images flow directly into confirmed video and anchor only their story. Preserve identity while actions and expressions vary. The user reviews results; no automatic repairs.

道具只按剧情出现，记录持有者、手别、数量、轮廓、状态和可见交接；先接触再产生物理效果。串位风险高时用独立建立镜头，不用固定武器或火把模板。
Only plot-needed props appear. Track owner, hand, count, silhouette, state and visible handoffs; contact precedes physical effect. Isolate risky staging rather than imposing a fixed weapon or torch scene.

## 声音、字幕与返修 / Audio, text and repair

短英文对白辅助画面，无默认旁白、字幕或画面标题。将末词完整和声线稳定写入创作要求，不增加听检或自动修音步骤。用户提出问题后按指定范围处理，保留已有版本。
Brief English supports the visuals without default narration, captions or titles. Request complete lines and stable voices in the brief; do not add listening checks or automatic sound repairs. Make changes only in response to user feedback and retain existing versions.
