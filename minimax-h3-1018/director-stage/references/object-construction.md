# Object construction / 物件搭建

Distinguish a supporting prop from a hero object. A supporting prop is read within the full shot: one rough construction pass should establish its silhouette, proportions and ground contact, with at most one correction if scene review exposes a problem. Close-up subjects, reference-matched objects and explicit fidelity requests justify staged construction. A simple hero object with few parts and no strong spatial constraints can still use a single pass.

## Layout, blockout, refinement

1. Study the reference or brief from overall proportion to major assemblies and then identity details. Name up to five features that make the object recognizable.
2. Before construction, write a part table: semantic name, rough shape, size, centre, orientation and attachment. Keep each assembly independently editable.
3. Check relative relationships. Vehicle wheel planes contain the travel direction and their axles are perpendicular to it; the frame connects them. Furniture supports meet the ground and tops remain horizontal. Symmetric parts mirror each other; attached parts actually meet. Confirm the editor's coordinate conventions rather than transferring those of another application.
4. Build a simple blockout, one readable approximation per part. Review front, side, top and three-quarter views together. Every view must agree with the same layout: a wheel appearing circular from one angle can still be incorrectly horizontal. For a longitudinal bicycle, the side shows both wheels and the frame, the front is narrow, and the top is a thin strip.
5. Resolve structural errors before detail. Refine the same object and part identities in this order: structure and joints, profiles and bevels, material contrast, small identifying details. Recheck layout after each pass so refinement does not regress it.

## Acceptance and recovery

The defining features must read correctly in every relevant captured view. Resolve disconnected parts, wrong axes and broken support relationships. Similarity metrics may support a comparison at a matching view but cannot establish material quality or full 3D fidelity. Isolated object review cannot replace inspection of its placement in the whole scene.

Classify a mismatch before correcting it. Wrong axes, missing parts or incorrect attachment return to the part plan and blockout; material, size and detail adjustments can remain local. Start with about three blockout and three refinement iterations for a complex hero object, unless the user gives another budget. At the limit, deliver an explicit list of unresolved mismatches rather than a completion claim.

## 中文要点

区分配景与主角物件。配景在整镜中判断，一次粗搭建立轮廓、比例和落地关系；场景验收发现问题时至多再做一次修正。特写、参考还原或明确保真要求适合分阶段制作；部件少且空间约束简单的主体仍可一次完成。

先分析整体比例、主要组件与识别细节，列出最多五项识别特征，再写部件表：语义名称、近似形状、尺寸、中心、朝向和连接位置，并保留独立编辑能力。车辆轮面包含行驶方向，轮轴与之垂直，车架与车轮相连；家具支撑落地，台面水平，对称部件镜像，连接处真实接触。坐标约定以当前编辑器为准。

先白模，后细节。将正面、侧面、顶面和斜视图一起检查，同一布局应在所有视角成立。自行车侧面应见两轮与车架，正面狭窄，顶面呈细条；单视角圆形不能证明车轮方向正确。结构通过后保留原对象和部件身份，依次细化连接结构、轮廓倒角、材质对比和识别细节，每轮重查布局。

识别特征须在相关视图中正确，断连、错轴和失去支撑必须处理。相似度仅作同角度比较的辅助，不能证明材质与三维保真；孤立物件检查也不能代替整场景验收。结构问题回到方案与白模，材质、尺寸和细节局部调整。复杂主体可先按三轮白模、三轮细化安排，用户预算优先；耗尽后列出未解决差异，不冒称完成。
