# Motion craft / 动作方法

Reuse a suitable existing motion when it communicates the beat. For a custom performance, inspect the closest available motion and adapt it rather than inventing an unsupported control language.

A readable action usually has two to five beats: anticipation, action and settlement. Gesture transitions settle, launches accelerate decisively, impacts punctuate, locomotion flows, and mechanical motion can remain even. Confirm what the editor means by holding, resetting and absolute versus relative poses before editing; a control labelled “hold” may reset to a base pose rather than freeze the current one.

Grounding matters more than flourish. Squatting and kneeling coordinate hips, knees, ankles and centre-of-mass lowering. Separate travel through the scene from in-place skeletal acting, then align them so feet do not slide. Joint-limit warnings require inspection when clamping changes the intended gesture.

Arrange clips as an intentional sequence. An overlap can accidentally double an action; a gap can freeze a character mid-stride. The end of the timeline must retain the requested final performance, and the static pose should agree with it rather than pop back visibly.

Inspect representative moments and playback where possible. A sequence of valid keyframes does not prove natural motion between them.

## 中文要点

已有动作能够表达节点时优先复用。自定义表演先查看最相近的动作再调整，不编造控件语法。

动作通常分为两到五个清晰节点：预备、执行、落定。姿态过渡要收稳，启动要果断，冲击有顿点，行走连贯，机械运动可匀速。编辑前确认保持、重置、绝对姿态和相对姿态的实际含义；“保持”控件可能回到基础姿态而不是冻结当前动作。

蹲跪需协调髋、膝、踝和重心下降。空间位移与原地骨骼表演分别设计后对齐，避免滑步。关节限幅改变动作意图时必须查看效果。片段重叠可能重复叠加动作，空档可能停在半步；结尾须停留在要求的表演，静态姿态与之相符，避免跳变。

检查代表性时刻，能够播放时检查连续播放。关键帧有效不等于中间运动自然。
