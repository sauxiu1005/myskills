# Blocking and composition / 摆位与构图

## Establish relationships before coordinates

Plan the primary subject, secondary subject, eyelines, foreground and background before assigning positions. Maintain a small relationship ledger for reference matching: left/right, front/back, support, attachment, occlusion and relative screen size. A plausible single view does not establish hidden geometry.

Create the environment, major masses, foreground props and characters before refining cameras. An exterior scene needs a depth cue beyond its named props: a facade, tree line or distant wall makes the place readable. Abstract shapes are sufficient for unresolved background masses. Recognizable objects seen at medium distance or closer need coherent, semantically correct models.

## Contact and facing

- Standing and walking feet meet the ground. Sitting hips meet the actual seat surface, including its scale; floor placement is not chair contact.
- A seated character faces with the seat. A conversation, bow or hand-off is oriented toward the other participant rather than their back.
- Use approximately 1.2–1.6 metres between adults for ordinary dialogue as a starting point; dramatic intent and framing may require a different distance.
- Avoid uniform crowd grids. Vary spacing, depth and facing while keeping silhouettes distinct.
- Moving subjects need a believable arrival direction. The last segment of travel should approach the partner along the intended facing, and the static scene should agree with the closing pose.

Inspect feet, seat contact, furniture intersections, reversed eyelines, merged silhouettes, foreground occlusion and empty areas in the actual shot. Coordinates alone do not establish these relationships.

## 中文要点

先写主次主体、视线、前后景与空间关系，再决定坐标。按参考还原时记录左右、前后、支撑、贴附、遮挡和画面占比；单一视角合理不代表隐藏结构已知。

先建立环境与大体块，再加前景道具和角色。外景应有立面、树线或远墙等纵深线索。远景抽象体块可以简化；中近景可辨识物件应保持完整模型与真实语义。

站立和行走的脚落地；坐姿臀部接触缩放后的实际座面并与座椅同向。交谈、鞠躬和递物应朝向对方正面。成人对话可先以约 1.2–1.6 米间距试摆，再按戏剧意图和构图调整。人群避免整齐网格，变化间距、纵深与朝向。移动最后一段应形成正确到达方向，静态场面与结尾姿态一致。

实际看图检查悬脚、座面接触、穿模、反向视线、剪影重叠、遮挡和空洞区域，不能仅凭坐标判定正确。
