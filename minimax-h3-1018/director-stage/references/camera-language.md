# Camera language / 镜头语言

Choose the narrative change first: reveal information, close emotional distance, follow action or change the perceived balance of power. A push-in emphasizes; a pull-out reveals context or isolation; lateral movement creates parallax; a crane changes scale or status; an orbit reveals form and relationships. Avoid combining moves merely because they are available.

Read actual subject positions before planning a camera path. Track a travelling subject when following its approach; a fixed look point at the arrival mark may leave it out of frame for most of the shot. With one seated and one travelling actor, a composition point between them or biased toward the entry side can include both. Inspect an early frame to confirm.

For one narrative beat, prefer a simple move. For a multi-beat shot, use a clear starting frame, two to four readable movement segments, and deliberate holds. A curved transition is generally easier to read than a string of abrupt corners. Keep foreground parallax without hiding the subject.

Coordinate camera beats with action: an arrival, reveal and camera hold should agree in time. A push-in must stop while the subject remains recognizable; filling the whole frame with one vehicle surface loses the intended shot. Preserve headroom, eyeline and silhouette separation through the move, not only at its end.

When revising, preserve the shot's identity and timeline placement. Restructuring a path does not inherently require changing its timing or creating a duplicate. Review start and end framing, intermediate visibility, camera height and lens distortion.

## 中文要点

先决定运镜揭示的信息或情绪变化。推进强调，拉远交代环境或孤立感，横移产生视差，升降改变尺度和地位，环绕揭示形体关系；不因功能可用就叠加运动。

按主体实际位置设计镜头。跟随移动主体时持续照顾其位置，不只瞄准终点；一坐一动的双人场景可把构图重点放在两人之间或偏向入画侧，并检查早期画面。

单节点优先简单运镜；多节点镜头先定起幅，再安排两到四段清晰运动和停顿，用平滑曲线避免突兀拐角。前景视差不能遮住主体。主体到达、信息揭示与镜头停顿应对齐。推进终点仍需让主体可辨识，不能被物体表面填满画面。全过程检查头顶空间、视线和剪影分离。

续改保留镜头身份与时间线位置，改路径不必另建重复镜头。检查起落幅、中段可见性、机位高度及透视变形。
