# Evidence and acceptance

Use within step 4 to plan checks and step 6 to inspect clips and final delivery. This reference adds no authorization checkpoint.

## Evidence record

For each key interval record file/version, time range, requirement, evidence method, observation, status and unresolved question. Status: observed compliance, clear defect, approved editorial omission, or unverified. Generated, inspected and user-accepted remain distinct.

File metadata establishes dimensions, duration, format and track presence. Semantic observation establishes visible action, relationships, composition and audible content; neither replaces the other. Inspect the opening, meaningful action/contact, camera transitions, convergence, actual freeze, transformation and ending as relevant. Reuse sufficient observations; a final frame cannot establish whole-clip continuity.

## Route-specific acceptance

For route A, separately observe low foreground travel, approach, real close orbit, natural principal-subject actions/gaze/expression, continuous pull-back, composition arrival and freeze. Require travel, changed viewing surfaces and background occlusion/parallax together; mere unfolding, minor parallax, zoom, pan, subject rotation or push/pull is insufficient. Check the agreed arc without inventing exact measured degrees. If one take was approved, inspect unintended cuts. Unpeopled art uses object geometry and appropriate environmental change instead of actors.

For route B, observe a meaningfully different opening, active actions and visible consequences, actual cuts, informative changes of viewpoint/shot scale, cross-cut inheritance and narrative arrival at the painting. File names, prompt text or target shot counts do not prove actual editing. Reject repetitive views of the same static pose or an unperformed action hidden by a cut, splash or occlusion.

## Observable tests

- **Action:** compare starting state, actual change, response and ending state. Clothing, hair or blinking cannot replace requested active movement. For landscapes/still lifes, inspect the approved environmental change instead of imposing people.
- **Camera:** inspect continuous parallax and spatial relationships across the path. Panning, lateral travel or subject rotation alone does not establish orbiting. “A visible arc” is not a measured exact angle; mark unmeasured angles accordingly. Check collisions, unintended cuts and continuation seams.
- **Identity and props:** verify subject count, appearance, costume, holders and functional surfaces. Distinguish anatomical left/right from screen sides. Check receive-before-release, support and visible handoffs; occlusion does not prove contact. Resolve conflicting hand reports with relevant excerpts and frames, leaving hidden facts unknown.
- **Audio:** inspect the actual final soundtrack independently when music or voice is disputed, not just the source clip. Check required/forbidden components, dialogue wording/intelligibility, synchronization, loop seams, fade and measured duration. Track presence or an audio-enabled flag does not prove content. No dialogue is not necessarily no human vocalization: gasps, sighs and breathing may violate a no-voice requirement. Birdsong, score and ambient beds follow the approved sound plan, not a universal ban. Do not infer speech from lip motion or remove required event sounds merely to obtain silence; do not overwrite the full sound design with an old wind/water-only track.
- **Ending:** obtain actual settling/freeze and transition frames and compare with the approved image/composition. Check pose, hands, gaze, objects, scale, framing, full-frame stability and ghosting. A static endpoint reference does not prove dynamic alignment. In actual-artwork mode verify the real file's deterministic placement, permitted borders/extension and seams; never certify pixel identity without an appropriate comparison. In painterly mode verify protected composition and edge content across the entire restyled frame, without unapproved artwork swaps, dimming or borders. An executed filter is not evidence of semantic restyling. Check for premature paint texture or still-image replacement during the intended live-action interval. Frozen transformation counts toward static-ending duration, not only the completed painting hold; verify the actual final timeline.
- **Final file:** measure actual assembled duration, dimensions, format and tracks; inspect the exported visual and audio result. Planned seconds and successful render status are insufficient. Check sources/dramatization and any requested text; no invented quotation is authentic.

## Conflicting or incomplete evidence

Keep conflicting reports and identify the precise unresolved claim. Recheck the smallest relevant interval, actual frames, independent final audio or file facts. Do not choose the most optimistic report. For timeout, truncation or conflicting reports, retain unknowns. If tools fail, vary the evidence request rather than repeat the same failing full-clip analysis; inspection failure does not authorize regeneration. State unresolved uncertainty and the actual inspection coverage. “No obvious misalignment” does not mean “perfect reconstruction.”

## Issue reporting and execution boundaries

Follow main step 6: inspection reports compliance, defects and unknowns; it does not trigger repair or regeneration. Preserve the actual file/version and report time, expected/actual behavior and impact. Wait for explicit instructions before repeating generation or replacing clips/audio, paid or free; no correction allowance or automatic recovery sequence applies. Prompt revision does not authorize media production. Only already approved routine post-production may continue within its explicit scope, without concealing missing action. When the user separately authorizes a change, inspect the affected portions, joins and final export. Keep intermediate/rejected assets distinct and do not claim acceptance of unresolved results.
